This study describes the effects of the insertion of ISAba1 in the intrinsic bla ADC gene of an Acinetobacter baumannii isolate showing co-resistance to carbapenems and colistin. Isolate 52971 showed low ceftazidime MIC values due to the disruption of bla ADC-195 by this insertion sequence.
